Conditions d'utilisation

Book2Market stores captured/imported book photos, generated PDFs, generated photo ZIP files, listing fields, and batch history locally on the user's device. Google Drive export is optional. When the user chooses Drive export, the app uses Google Sign-In and the Drive drive.file scope to create or reuse a visible /Book2Market folder in the user's Google Drive and upload app-created files for the selected book listing packet. The app does not operate a backend service for user accounts or remote storage. It does not log in to Marketplace, publish listings, scrape Marketplace pages, relist items, or send messages.
